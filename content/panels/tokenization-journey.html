Tokenization Journey — Phase Log

A living record of proving the “numbers as tokens” premise, phase by phase. The Flow and Metrics panels are timeless; this one updates. Each phase's numbers are stamped with the sample they came from, because readings change between rounds even when the framework does not.

  • Phase 1 — Discretization sanity

    Done

    Does binning a continuous feature into tokens preserve its label information? Run the gate on a labeled signal+background sample across all four lanes (nn1/nn2 × barrel/endcap).

  • Phase 2 — Early snapshot + cut grammar

    Next

    One cutflow edit moves the snapshot upstream of the selective cuts and adds per-cut flags. That yields a clean, region-matched re-measurement (separation that is feature-intrinsic, not kinematic), unblocks the BiGram grammar gate, and gives the sample the $H$/$C$ machinery needs. Re-run across all four signal mass points.

  • Phase 3 — Vectorization (Evt2Vec)

    Later

    Turn the validated binning into an honest tokenizer (freeze edges, namespace, structural tokens, ordering) and learn the embedding via InfoNCE — which also supplies the $I_\text{NCE}$ lower bound of the capacity bracket. Gate to CutFormer.

Illustrative readings (how the Metrics panel maps onto real numbers): the strongest separator htmiss_NOSYS is also the worst MI-retainer (retention 0.67) — it carries the most information (mi_raw 0.091 vs ~0.04 elsewhere), so 16 bins shed the most absolute MI even as AUC barely moves. The isolation features collapse hardest (sumTrackPt0p2Cone max_bin_frac 0.81 — 81% of events in one bin), being zero-inflated.

Round-1 caveats — these numbers are a first pass, not the result.
  • AUCs are inflated by region mismatch. VR is HTmiss < 40 GeV; signal “all” spans 0–700. So much of the apparent separation is kinematic, not feature-intrinsic. The retention conclusion is robust to this; the AUC magnitudes are not physics discrimination.
  • Single mass point. mS35 only — mass-point sensitivity deferred to the four-point re-run.
  • Barrel is borderline-stat (263 signal rows, just over the 200 floor).
  • Post-NN sample. Survivors, not the process — no cut grammar and no per-stage floor are computable here. That is exactly what Phase 2 fixes.

What Phase 1 establishes: the tokenization step is information-preserving in-domain on ATLAS data — the DPI step is lossless. What it does not: clean feature-only separation, mass-point generality, cut grammar, or any capacity/floor number. Those wait on Phase 2.
